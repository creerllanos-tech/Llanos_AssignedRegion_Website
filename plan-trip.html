Sample 3-day itinerary

Three-day itinerary for Northern Mindanao
Day Morning Afternoon Evening
Day 1 Arrive at Laguindingan Airport and transfer to Cagayan de Oro Whitewater rafting on the Cagayan de Oro River Dinner and rest in the city
Day 2 Travel to Iligan City Maria Cristina Falls and Tinago Falls Return to Cagayan de Oro
Day 3 Travel to Camiguin by ferry Island hopping to Mantigue Island Sunset by the coast, then head home

Travel tips

  • Fly into Laguindingan Airport, then take a bus or van to Cagayan de Oro.
  • Book ferry rides to Camiguin early during holidays and festivals.
  • Check the weather before island trips.
  • Bring some cash, because small towns may not have ATMs.
  • Use licensed guides for rafting and mountain hikes.
