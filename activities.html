Activities & Experiences

Pick a category and plan your days.

Adventure

  • Raft the rapids of the Cagayan de Oro River
  • Try zip lines and rides at Dahilayan Adventure Park in Bukidnon
  • Hike the slopes of Mt. Hibok-Hibok with a local guide

Nature & Beaches

  • Go island hopping and snorkeling around Camiguin
  • Swim and relax on the sand at Mantigue Island
  • Visit Maria Cristina Falls and Tinago Falls in Iligan City

Culture

  • Join the Kaamulan Festival in Bukidnon
  • Celebrate the Lanzones Festival in Camiguin
  • Watch the street dancing at the Higalaay Festival in Cagayan de Oro

Food

  • Buy pastel, Camiguin's soft bread with a sweet filling
  • Taste fresh lanzones in season
  • Enjoy grilled seafood at a seaside restaurant
  • Try fresh pineapple grown in Bukidnon
