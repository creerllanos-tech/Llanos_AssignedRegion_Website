Popular Destinations

Beaches, mountains, waterfalls, and rivers across Northern Mindanao.

Mantigue Island

A small island ringed by white sand, with a coral reef and marine sanctuary just offshore. It is a favorite for snorkeling and quiet beach days.

Location: Mahinog, Camiguin

Camiguin Coastline

Calm, clear water along the Camiguin coast, with the island's volcanic peaks rising behind. Early mornings here are cool and quiet.

Location: Camiguin

Mt. Hibok-Hibok

Camiguin's best-known volcano, with green farmland at its base. Guided hikes lead up its slopes for wide views of the island.

Location: Mambajao, Camiguin

Mt. Balatukan Cafe

A highland garden cafe with a peacock-shaped hedge arch, flower beds, and wooden cottages. A cool, relaxing stop with coffee and mountain air.

Location: Misamis Oriental

Maria Cristina Falls

One of the best-known waterfalls in the country, dropping more than 90 meters into a gorge. It also supplies hydroelectric power to the region.

Location: Iligan City, Lanao del Norte

Cagayan de Oro Whitewater Rafting

Paddle down the Cagayan de Oro River through rapids for beginners and thrill seekers alike. Guides and safety gear are included in most tours.

Location: Cagayan de Oro City, Misamis Oriental
