Welcome to Region X

A region full of natural wonders

Welcome to Northern Mindanao (Region X), a beautiful region in the Philippines known for its stunning natural attractions, rich culture, and warm hospitality. Located in the northern part of Mindanao, the region is composed of five provinces: Bukidnon, Camiguin, Lanao del Norte, Misamis Occidental, and Misamis Oriental.

Beyond its natural beauty, Northern Mindanao is also home to vibrant festivals, fascinating history, and delicious local delicacies. Visitors can explore the famous Maria Cristina Falls in Iligan City, enjoy the beaches of Camiguin Island, experience whitewater rafting in Cagayan de Oro City, or discover the traditions of Bukidnon's indigenous communities. Discover the beauty, culture, and adventures of Northern Mindanao, where every destination has a story to tell!
