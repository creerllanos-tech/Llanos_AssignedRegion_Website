Our History & Culture

The people, stories, and traditions behind the region.

A brief history

Beyond its breathtaking natural beauty, Northern Mindanao is also a region rich in vibrant festivals, fascinating history, diverse traditions, and delicious local delicacies. Located in the northern part of Mindanao in the Philippines, this beautiful region offers a unique combination of natural wonders, exciting adventures, and meaningful cultural experiences. One of its most famous attractions is Maria Cristina Falls in Iligan City, known for its impressive height, powerful flow, and breathtaking scenery. Another must-visit destination is Camiguin Island, often called the “Island Born of Fire,” which is famous for its pristine beaches, volcanic landscapes, refreshing hot and cold springs, and the historic Sunken Cemetery. Visitors can also enjoy the island's peaceful atmosphere, explore its natural wonders, and taste its sweet lanzones, one of its most beloved local products.

For adventure seekers, Cagayan de Oro City offers thrilling outdoor activities, especially whitewater rafting along the Cagayan de Oro River, where visitors can experience the excitement of navigating rushing currents and rocky passages. Meanwhile, the province of Bukidnon welcomes travelers with its cool climate, rolling hills, vast pineapple plantations, and magnificent mountain views. It is also home to indigenous communities, including the Talaandig, Higaonon, and Bukidnon peoples, whose traditional dances, music, crafts, beliefs, and stories contribute to the region's rich cultural heritage. Northern Mindanao also celebrates its identity through colorful festivals, such as the Higalaay Festival in Cagayan de Oro City and the Kaamulan Festival in Bukidnon. These celebrations feature lively street dancing, traditional performances, local products, and community gatherings that showcase the unity, creativity, and hospitality of the people. In addition, visitors can explore historical landmarks, old churches, museums, and heritage sites that reveal the region's fascinating past and the cultural influences that have shaped its communities.

Aside from its natural attractions and cultural experiences, Northern Mindanao is also a wonderful destination for food lovers. The region offers a variety of local delicacies, fresh seafood, tropical fruits, traditional dishes, and other regional specialties that reflect its agricultural abundance and diverse cultural influences. Exploring local markets and eateries allows visitors to discover authentic Filipino flavors while experiencing the warmth and friendliness of the local people. Whether you are an adventure seeker looking for exciting outdoor activities, a nature lover eager to explore spectacular landscapes, a history enthusiast interested in discovering the past, or a traveler hoping to experience Filipino culture and traditions, Northern Mindanao has something special to offer. With its magnificent waterfalls, beautiful islands, scenic mountains, exciting adventures, colorful festivals, and rich culinary heritage, the region promises unforgettable experiences for every visitor. Discover the beauty, culture, and adventures of Northern Mindanao, where every destination has a story to tell and every journey brings you closer to the heart of the Philippines!

Cultural highlights

Festivals

  • Kaamulan Festival in Bukidnon honors the region's indigenous communities.
  • Lanzones Festival in Camiguin celebrates the harvest of lanzones.
  • Higalaay Festival in Cagayan de Oro honors the city's patron saint.

Traditional crafts and foods

  • Handwoven baskets and textiles made by indigenous communities
  • Bamboo and rattan crafts
  • Pastel, a soft bread with a sweet filling, from Camiguin
  • Lanzones and pineapple, local fruit favorites
